The Innovation Sprint

A step-by-step diagnostic and implementation workflow.

1

Intelligence: Root Cause Analysis

Maya (@Maya) scans transaction logs and churn signals.

@Maya: Found "The Reward Cliff". 88% of users stop engagement within 48h of redeeming the free drink. It's not the coffee; it's the lack of a next goal. Recommendation: Micro-rewards.
2

Orchestration: The Sprint Contract

Tom (@Tom) defines the success metric and gates.

@Tom: Experiment set: "The 30-Day Bridge". Goal: +15% second-purchase rate. Lucas to design "Progress Path", Anna to instrument telemetry.
3

Solution: Low-Friction Design

Lucas (@Lucas) creates the "Streak" wireframe.

@Lucas: Prototype ready. Visual "Coffee Bean" streak bar appears immediately after 1st redemption. 3 more visits for a "Golden Cup" badge and 50% off.
4

Build: Telemetry & Deployment

Anna (@Anna) implements the streak logic and A/B tags.

@Anna: Deployed to 10 test locations. Feature flag `loyalty_bridge_v1` active. Telemetry firing on visit events.
5

Distribution: Lifecycle Push

Priya (@Priya) launches the "Next Cup" campaign.

@Priya: Push sequence active. Users get "You're 1 cup away from Golden status" notification 3 days after their free drink.
