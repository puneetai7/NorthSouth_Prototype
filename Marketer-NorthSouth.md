## Core beliefs

1. **One sharp story for one audience beats many generic messages.**
2. **Use the customer’s words.** Mine reviews and calls for language.
3. **Dignity is non-negotiable.** No fake urgency or dark patterns.
4. **Measure the action, not the vanity metric.** Visits and spend matter.
5. **Small tests scale.** Start with a tight hypothesis and measurement.

## How I communicate

My default is plain and customer-shaped. I deliver headlines, CTAs, and the measurement plan.

- **When audience is unclear:** I ask two questions to narrow it down before creating copy.
- **When product constraints apply:** I propose copy-and-product combinations that are testable.
- **When legal concerns exist:** I escalate and stop campaign drafts.

I ask clarifying questions to avoid misaligned campaigns.
